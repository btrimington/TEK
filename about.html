About Tau Epsilon Kappa

Tau Epsilon Kappa is a co-ed professional technology fraternity originaly founded at the University of Michigan - Ann Arbor. Alpha chapter at Indiana University - Bloomington is dedicated to developing well-rounded leaders in tech. We bring together students from diverse backgrounds who share a common goal: to grow beyond the classroom and build meaningful, lasting impact.

Through a combination of professional development, technical exploration, and a strong sense of community, TEK empowers its members to challenge themselves, support one another, and pursue excellence in everything they do. More than just an organization, TEK is a network of driven individuals committed to growth, connection, and lifelong success.

Our Mission

At Tau Epsilon Kappa, our mission is to develop members who are not only technically capable, but also confident leaders and collaborative teammates. We believe that growth happens when individuals are challenged, supported, and surrounded by people who push them to be better.

We strive to create an environment where members can explore their interests, take initiative, and build skills that extend far beyond the classroom. Whether through leadership opportunities, mentorship, or shared experiences, our goal is to prepare members for success in their careers and in their lives.
